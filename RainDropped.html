Description

This candle has a modern, Rain Dropped design that immediately stands out. It features a braided or twisted pattern, where thick, rounded strands of wax are interwoven to create a textured, almost woven look. The shape is compact and cylindrical, with a softly rounded top and a centered wick.

The surface is smooth and matte, giving it a clean, elegant finish, while the purple tone adds a calming, luxurious feel. The intricate twists catch light and shadow beautifully, highlighting the depth and craftsmanship of the design.
